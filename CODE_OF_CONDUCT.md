# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a place to learn and build together. Everyone taking part is expected to be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work.

Examples of behavior we welcome:

* Clear, constructive feedback on code and ideas.
* Patience with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
* Crediting other people's work and ideas.

Examples of behavior we do not accept:

* Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
* Discrimination or demeaning comments based on who someone is.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project uses: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any event where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern to **conduct@penti.ai**. Reports go to the project maintainer, stay confidential, and get a response within 5 business days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that breaks this code, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior continues. Decisions are made by the maintainer listed in CODEOWNERS.
